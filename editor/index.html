How building works (2 minutes)
  1. On GitHub, press Fork on the WristBlocks page to get your own copy (free account).
  2. In your copy, open the Actions tab once and press the green button to turn builds on.
  3. Type your copy's name above (like your-name/wristblocks) and press Build my watch app.
  4. GitHub opens with your app file ready. Press Commit changes.
  5. About 10 minutes later your apps appear under Releases in your copy: one for Apple Watch, one for Wear OS.
